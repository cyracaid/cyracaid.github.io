Selected Research
Research question
A wrongly entered value, silently corrected by a parser, can look perfectly legitimate. How do you clean self-reported data without hiding what was changed?
Approach
An open-source, human-in-the-loop R pipeline where every correction is logged and reviewable, validated on injected, real, and public data.
Where
Stanford Psychophysiology Lab, home sleep study.
Output
Manuscript in preparation.
Research question
When a model predicts what trained observers rate, what information are its predictions really reflecting?
Approach
An audit of multimodal AI scoring of therapists’ interpersonal skills on teletherapy trial recordings, combining text, voice, and facial expression, checked against observer ratings.
Where
Penn Medicine, Department of Psychiatry.
Output
Manuscript in preparation.
Research question
When a bodily signal and a verbal report diverge, is one of them wrong, or is the divergence itself the signal?
Approach
An online interoception task with webcam-based heart-rate estimation, paired with participants’ descriptions of recalled experiences. Does spontaneous language capture what ratings miss?
Where
Embodied Computation Group, Aarhus University & Cambridge Psychiatry.
Output
Pilot in development.
