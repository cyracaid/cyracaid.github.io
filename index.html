Research Overview

My research sits at the intersection of clinical psychology, affective neuroscience, and computational methods. I study how people perceive their own bodies and emotions — and how the gap between actual and perceived feeling relates to anxiety, depression, and social cognition. My current work focuses on interoceptive metacognition as a transdiagnostic mechanism underlying emotional disorders.

Outside of research, I enjoy reading and writing literature, photography, fashion, and playing video games.
